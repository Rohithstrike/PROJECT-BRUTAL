WEEKLY SPLIT — WHY THIS ORDER

Mon → Sat · Sun recovery
Concurrent-training logic: strength first, cardio after on lifting days. Hard intervals never precede heavy lower-body work. Wednesday conditioning is low-impact bike/row after legs; Friday HYROX keeps leg loading moderate (sled/lunge volume is capped) so Saturday's hinge-dominant session targets a different pattern. Skipping sits on upper-body days only.

10,000 STEPS

An activity target, not a magic fat-loss threshold. Daily movement adds meaningful energy expenditure without the recovery cost of extra hard cardio.

  • Normal days: ~10,000 steps (training-session steps count).
  • Wed & Sat (lower body): 8,000–10,000 is fine if legs are heavy.
  • Sunday: easy walking keeps activity up and aids recovery.
  • Never chase steps at the expense of sleep or session quality.

OPTIONAL MORNING PROTOCOL

Never mandatory. The program works perfectly with one session per day.

  • 10–30 min easy walk (fasted or fed — preference).
  • Or 20–30 min Zone 2 bike/incline walk on Mon/Tue/Thu only.
  • 5–10 min mobility: hips, T-spine, ankles, breathing.
  • Avoid: hard intervals before Wed/Sat legs, extra lifting, double hard sessions.
  • Skip it entirely in YELLOW/RED readiness.

Heavy compounds RIR 2–3 · Moderate hypertrophy RIR 1–2 · Isolation RIR 0–2 · Conditioning by RPE · Deload RIR 3–4. Most work is not taken to RIR 0.

LOAD CLASSES

  • HEAVY ~3–8 reps: technique, strength, long rest, RIR 2–3.
  • MODERATE ~6–12 reps: core hypertrophy work, RIR 1–2.
  • HIGH REP ~12–25+: isolation, pump, endurance, RIR 0–2.
  • POWER low reps, full recovery, never fatigued.

Conditioning: progress duration → density → output. Running: conservatively (walk/run → continuous → intervals). HYROX: total work & transitions before intensity. Isolation: reps first, then load.

INTENSITY TECHNIQUES (LABELLED IN SESSIONS)

  • ANTAGONIST SUPERSET — opposing muscles back-to-back (e.g. curls + rear delts); saves time, never on heavy compounds.
  • OPTIONAL DROP SET — FINAL SET ONLY — isolation work, selected weeks only.
  • REST-PAUSE — OPTIONAL — final set of isolation: reach target RIR, 15 s breath, 2–4 more reps.

WHEN NOT TO PROGRESS

  • Bar path, depth or posture breaks down.
  • Joint pain (not muscular effort) appears — swap to the listed substitute.
  • Readiness is YELLOW/RED — hold load, reduce sets.
  • Performance drops 2+ sessions in a row → fatigue check before pushing.

Train hard · but manage fatigue

Recovery / Rules

Adaptation happens between sessions. These rules protect the process.

GREEN

Train normally

  • Slept ≥7 h, motivation normal
  • Soreness mild, warm-up feels normal
  • Loads/reps on track
YELLOW

Reduce

  • Sets −20–30 % or load slightly lower or stop 1–2 reps further from failure
  • Triggers: poor sleep, heavy soreness, low drive, morning resting HR noticeably above your normal (if tracked)
  • Conditioning drops to easy RPE 3–4
RED

Recovery session or rest

  • Unusual fatigue, illness-like symptoms, persistent performance decline, pain
  • 20–30 min easy walk + mobility, or full rest
  • Never train through serious pain

SUNDAY RECOVERY

  • Easy walking 30–60 min accumulated (steps 8–10k)
  • 10–15 min mobility: hips, ankles, T-spine, pecs/lats
  • Weekly waist measurement + body-weight weekly average review
  • Progress photos every 2 weeks (same light, time, pose)
  • Plan the week: food prep, sleep window, session times

SLEEP · WARM-UP · COOL-DOWN

  • Sleep target ~7.5–9 h where possible; consistent wake time
  • Warm-up every session: 5–8 min easy cardio + 3–5 min activation + ramp sets on the first lift
  • Cool-down 2–5 min: walk + nasal breathing + 1–2 stretches for the trained area
  • DOMS: easy movement beats complete rest; it is not a measure of session quality

DELOAD STRATEGY

  • Week 4 & 8: built-in consolidation (~10–25 % less volume, RIR higher). Go lighter if readiness is poor.
  • Week 13: full deload + assessment — sets roughly halved, RIR 3–4, movement patterns kept, some intensity kept, conditioning reduced.
  • Deload ≠ doing nothing. It lets fatigue dissipate so fitness shows.
  • Unplanned deload: 3+ RED/YELLOW days in a week.

CONCURRENT TRAINING RULES

  • Strength first, cardio after on lifting days.
  • Friday reverses priority: conditioning is the main goal.
  • No hard running intervals within 24 h before heavy legs.
  • Most cardio easy; 1–2 harder exposures per week.
  • Skipping only on upper-body days, short bouts.

FAT LOSS & NUTRITION — ESSENTIALS

Concise by design
  • Spot reduction is not possible. Trunk fat drops with overall fat loss, which requires a sustained energy deficit.
  • BMR ≠ maintenance. InBody BMR (1,841 kcal) is energy at complete rest.
  • Rough maintenance estimate = BMR × activity factor. For a 6-day trainee at ~10k steps a factor of ~1.5–1.7 is a common starting assumption → ≈2,750–3,100 kcal. Treat it as a starting guess only.
  • Run 2–3 weeks, watch the weekly-average weight trend, then adjust. A modest deficit (≈300–500 kcal below real maintenance, or roughly 0.5–1 % body weight lost per week) protects muscle and performance.
  • Protein: commonly ~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, spread over 3–5 meals.
  • Fibre: vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains daily.
  • Hydration: drink regularly; more around training and heat. Never dehydrate for a weigh-in.
  • Mostly minimally processed foods; consistent meal structure; carbs around training support performance.
  • Never: starvation, crash diets, dehydration, extreme cardio, or unsafe stimulants. Consult a registered dietitian/doctor for medical needs.
